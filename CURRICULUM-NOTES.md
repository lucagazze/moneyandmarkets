## Source authors/systems, as given by Bruno

1. **Jesse Livermore** — *Reminiscences of a Stock Operator* / Principios de especulación.
   Trade with the trend, wait for confirmation before entering, cut losses fast, let winners run.
   Core idea: don't predict the market, react correctly to it and manage risk.
2. **Richard D. Wyckoff** — Wyckoff Method / *Studies in Tape Reading*.
   Reads price + volume + supply/demand to spot large-player ("smart money") behavior.
   Four phases: accumulation, markup, distribution, markdown.
3. **Nicolas Darvas** — *How I Made $2,000,000 in the Stock Market* / Darvas Box.
   Identify stocks consolidating in a range ("box"); buy on breakout above the box top.
   Buys strength, does not try to predict the move.
4. **William J. O'Neil** — *How to Make Money in Stocks* / CAN SLIM.
   Combines fundamental + technical: earnings/sales growth, New products/management,
   Supply & demand, Leadership in sector, Institutional sponsorship, Market direction.
5. **David Ryan** — O'Neil method, growth & momentum investing in practice.
   Refined O'Neil's principles: leading stocks, constructive bases, breakouts on volume;
   strong emphasis on reading the general market before taking risk.
6. **Mark Minervini** — *Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard* / SEPA + VCP.
   SEPA (Specific Entry Point Analysis): growth stocks with strong trend + technically
   favorable entry. VCP (Volatility Contraction Pattern): volatility contracts within a
   consolidation before a breakout. Combines growth, momentum, structure, timing, strict
   risk management.
7. **Mark Minervini** — *Think & Trade Like a Champion*.
   Deepens SEPA with a focus on execution: stock selection, entries, cutting losses, managing
   winners. Core principle: asymmetry between risk and reward — small potential loss vs. large
   potential upside.
8. **Gil Morales & Chris Kacher** — *Trade Like an O'Neil Disciple*.
   Practical evolution of the O'Neil school: leaders, bases, breakouts, distribution signals,
   institutional behavior. Also covers bull AND bear market operation, including short-selling.
9. **Stan Weinstein** — *Secrets for Profiting in Bull and Bear Markets* / Stage Analysis.
   4 stages of a stock's cycle: Stage 1 (base), Stage 2 (advance), Stage 3 (distribution/top),
   Stage 4 (decline). Goal: know which stage a stock is in before deciding to take a position.
10. **Tom Williams** — *Master the Markets* / Volume Spread Analysis (VSA).
    Reads volume + price range + close together to infer supply/demand — accumulation,
    distribution, absorption, lack of institutional interest. Evolution of Wyckoff concepts.
11. **John J. Murphy** — *Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets*.
    Comprehensive classical-TA manual: trends, support/resistance, chart patterns, moving
    averages, indicators, volume, momentum, intermarket analysis. A knowledge base, not a
    single system.
12. **Robert D. Edwards & John Magee** — *Technical Analysis of Stock Trends*.
    A founding classic of Western technical analysis: trends, chart patterns, formations,
    support/resistance, breakouts. Systematizes the visual reading of price action.
13. **Mark Douglas** — *Trading in the Zone* — psychology of trading.
    Think in probabilities and in a series of trades, not certainty per trade. Discipline,
    accepting losses, emotional control, consistent execution of a system.
14. **Brett N. Steenbarger** — *The Psychology of Trading* / Performance Trading.
    Trading as a performance discipline: recognizing behavior patterns, improving decisions,
    building repeatable processes. Complements technical systems with the gap between HAVING
    a system and being able to EXECUTE it consistently.

## Bruno's own synthesis / how they relate

- Livermore → trend, timing, speculation, loss management.
- Wyckoff → supply, demand, volume, institutional behavior.
- Darvas → consolidations and breakouts via "boxes."
- O'Neil → growth + leadership + momentum + CAN SLIM.
- Ryan → practical application of the O'Neil school.
- Morales & Kacher → O'Neil taken to more advanced operation (incl. shorting).
- Weinstein → identifying which trend stage a stock is in.
- Minervini → selection + trend + VCP + entry point + risk.
- Tom Williams → advanced volume/price interpretation.
- Murphy / Edwards & Magee → the language and tools of technical analysis.
- Douglas / Steenbarger → psychology, discipline, execution.

**The common thread (Bruno's words):** Identify strength → wait for the right structure →
confirm demand → enter at controlled risk → let the trend develop → exit when the thesis
is invalidated.

Wyckoff + O'Neil + Minervini + Weinstein + Darvas + Livermore = the conceptual core.
VSA + classical TA (Murphy/Edwards & Magee) + psychology (Douglas/Steenbarger) = the
complementary toolkit.

## Proposed 7-part book structure (mirrors the synthesis above, mapped onto the site's
existing "7 parts" branding pattern)

1. **Foundations of Speculation** — Livermore: trend, timing, cutting losses, letting
   winners run. The philosophical starting point.
2. **Reading Supply and Demand** — Wyckoff's four phases + Tom Williams' VSA as the modern
   deep-dive extension of the same idea.
3. **Stages and Structures** — Weinstein's Stage Analysis (1-4) + the Darvas Box — knowing
   where a stock is in its cycle before acting.
4. **The CAN SLIM System** — O'Neil's CAN SLIM + David Ryan's practical application —
   selecting growth/leadership stocks.
5. **Modern Momentum Trading** — Minervini's SEPA/VCP + Morales & Kacher's O'Neil-disciple
   playbook (bull AND bear markets, incl. shorting).
6. **The Language of Charts** — Murphy's Technical Analysis + Edwards & Magee's classical
   chart patterns — the shared vocabulary/toolkit.
7. **The Trader's Mind** — Douglas (probabilistic thinking, discipline) + Steenbarger
   (performance psychology, execution) — closing on psychology, same pattern as Physics
   Study Lab closing on Modern Physics.
