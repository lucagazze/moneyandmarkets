Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit financestudylab.com or buy our digital products, why we use it and what rights you have, including under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who we are

Bruno Gazze Gonzalez (trading as Finance Study Lab), Av. Madres Plaza 25 de Mayo 2880, Rosario, Santa Fe, CP 2000, Argentina is the controller of your personal data. Contact: info@financestudylab.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Order data: name, email address, country and purchase details.
  • Payment data: handled directly by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Browsing data: pages visited, device, approximate location from your IP address and how you interact with the site, collected with cookies and similar technologies.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email.

3. How we use it and our legal basis

  • To deliver your purchase and provide support (performance of a contract).
  • To process payments and refunds and prevent fraud (contract and legitimate interests).
  • To meet tax and accounting obligations (legal obligation).
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising (consent, where required).
  • To send you emails about our products, which you can unsubscribe from at any time (legitimate interests or consent).

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We only share it with service providers that help us run the business:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Our checkout provider — order processing and delivery.
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — measuring our advertising through the Meta Pixel.
  • Analytics providers — understanding how the site is used.

5. How long we keep it

Order records for as long as tax law requires. Marketing data until you unsubscribe. Analytics data for up to 26 months.

6. Security

We use providers who encrypt data in transit and at rest. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or move it elsewhere. Email info@financestudylab.com and we will reply within one month. If you are in the UK you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

8. Children

Our products are not intended for anyone under 18.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
